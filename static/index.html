How Advier Works

Bridging the gap between the public and legal practitioners through technology, security, and verification.

Verified Professionals

The Issue: Finding a genuine, qualified legal practitioner online can be full of uncertainty.

How We Resolve It: All advocates on Advier are required to provide their Bar Council Registration Number, which undergoes validation during registration. Clients can browse with full confidence.

Effortless Bookings

The Issue: Coordinating schedules, explaining cases, and sending background files via email is messy.

How We Resolve It: Advier offers a structured calendar system. Clients book dates, state issues, and upload case documents directly. Advocates receive instant notifications and accept or reschedule instantly.

Secure Communications

The Issue: Legal matters require confidentiality. Unencrypted channels risk exposing sensitive information.

How We Resolve It: Communications, data retrieval, and chat rooms on Advier are sealed with JWT Token Security, preventing data breaches and unauthorized URL bypasses. Your cases remain strictly confidential.
